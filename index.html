About Running Local

Running Local is an independent New York City newsroom. We make short vertical video reports that follow a public decision, such as a fare change, a Council hearing or a city mandate, to the people it affects.

Our stories are built for Instagram first and made to be understood with the sound off.

  • Sourced. Every claim is backed by a document, record or interview, and sources are listed with each post.
  • Independent. Running Local is not affiliated with any political party, campaign or advocacy group.
  • Funding. Running Local is independently funded and does not accept money from the people or organizations it covers.
  • Corrections. If we get something wrong, we fix it and say so. Write to hello@runninglocalnews.com.
